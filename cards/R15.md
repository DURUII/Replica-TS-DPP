# R15 — Eq (35): cancellation of the common terms in the penalty difference

**Status: CLOSED.**  `lean4/TSDPP/Model.lean` (+ `TSDPP/Regret.lean` consumes it)

## Statement

```
penalty_identity {N} (γ : Fin N → ℝ) (hγ : ∑ i, γ i = 1) (a : Fin N) (X Y : Fin N → ℕ) :
  ∑ i, γ i * (if i = a then (X i : ℝ) + 1 else (Y i : ℝ) + 1)
    = (∑ i, γ i * (Y i : ℝ)) + 1 + γ a * ((X a : ℝ) - (Y a : ℝ))

penalty_diff {N} (γ : Fin N → ℝ) (a : Fin N) (Yprev : Fin N → ℕ) (m m' : ℝ) :
  expPenalty γ a Yprev m - expPenalty γ a Yprev m' = γ a * (m - m')
```

## Consumes

R3 (Eq (9), the same `penalty_identity`), R8 (`bestArm_congr` — the *reason* the same arm `a`
appears on both sides).

## Consumed by

R10 (`perRoundPenaltyDiff_le` is `penalty_diff` composed with the mean bound).

## Proof route

`penalty_identity` splits the sum at `i = a` with `sum_ite_eq'` and uses `Σ γ_i = 1`.
`penalty_diff` is then pure `ring` after `unfold expPenalty`: every term not involving the pulled
arm is literally identical in the two subtrahends, so it cancels, and the `+1` cancels with itself.

## Notes — this is where Lemma 1 earns its keep

`penalty_diff` is only *usable* because Lemma 1 (`bestArm_congr`) guarantees the *same* arm is
indexed at the *same* history under both `θ*` and `θ`.  Without that, the two expectations would
be over different integrands and no cancellation would be available.  The paper uses Lemma 1
silently at Eq (35); this card records it as a real dependency rather than a remark.

## Open sub-nodes

None.  The `θ`-dependence has been fully pushed into the belief means of R5/R6, which is exactly
the paper's intent.
