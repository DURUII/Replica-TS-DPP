# R8 — Lemma 1: the belief-DPP policy is a deterministic policy mapping

**Status: CLOSED.**  `lean4/TSDPP/Model.lean`

## Statement

`bestArm idx = (Finset.univ.exists_max_image idx Finset.univ_nonempty).choose`
`bestArm_spec : ∀ i, idx i ≤ idx (bestArm idx)`
`bestArm_congr : idx = idx' → bestArm idx = bestArm idx'`

## Consumes

Nothing.

## Consumed by

R10 (the `A_t` in Eq (36) and Eq (37) is literally the same object under `θ*` and under `θ_l`).

## Proof route

`Finset.exists_max_image` on the finite arm set; the tie-break is `Classical.choose`.

## Notes

This is a small card but it is load-bearing: the paper's Eq (35) says "the same decision `A_t` is
generated, even though the transition probabilities may differ … because `π_l` is a deterministic
policy under the same history `H_{t−1}`".  `bestArm_congr` is the formal content of that sentence.
Because `bestArm` is a *function* of the index vector and the index vector is a function of the
history, the induced policy mapping is deterministic by construction.
