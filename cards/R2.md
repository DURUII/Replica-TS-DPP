# R2 — the copy-AoI recursion (paper Eq (4))

**Status: CLOSED.**  `lean4/TSDPP/Model.lean`

## Statement

`copyStep (sel : Bool) (Xprev Yprev : ℕ) = if sel then Xprev + 1 else Yprev + 1`
`copyStep_iterate : (fun y => copyStep false lam y)^[δ] lam = lam + δ`

## Consumes

Nothing (R1 in spirit, not in code).

## Consumed by

R10 (the record `(λ, δ)` of Lemma 2 is *defined* by this iteration).

## Proof route

## Notes

`copyStep_iterate` is the closed form the paper uses silently from Lemma 2 onwards: `δ` slots
after the arm was last pulled at state `λ`, the copy AoI is `λ + δ`.  Formalizing it makes
explicit that the "record" `(λ_i, δ_i)` determines the whole law — which is exactly the
preamble of Lemma 2.
